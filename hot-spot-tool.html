The Problem

Product images tell half the story

B&H sells complex, high-consideration gear — cameras, lenses, lighting rigs, audio equipment. Customers browsing a Product Detail Page, Image Gallery, or Explora article often can't tell what they're looking at without context. Specs live in a table. Features live in a paragraph. But the actual product sits in a static image with no explanation.

The existing options — custom dev work per image, manual graphic design, or leaving it to copy alone — were too slow and too expensive to scale across a catalog of thousands.

The insight: The gap wasn't content — it was surface. Customers are already looking at the image. Putting the annotation right there, on the product, eliminates the disconnect between what they see and what they need to know.

What I Built

Annotate once. Export anywhere.

The Annotation Suite is a browser-based tool that lets content and merchandising teams upload any product image and layer interactive annotations directly on top — then export a finished image ready for PDPs, Image Galleries, and Explora editorial content.

No design software. No engineering ticket. Upload, annotate, export.

📍

Hotspots

Pin numbered callouts to specific product features — customers tap or hover to reveal details right on the image.

📐

Measurements

Overlay precise dimension annotations on product images — critical for gear where size and form factor drive purchase decisions.

🖼

Infographics

Layer highlight zones and visual callouts to turn a product photo into a feature breakdown — ideal for Explora editorial content.

✏️

Callouts

Add labeled arrows pointing to controls, ports, or features — bridging the gap between what the image shows and what the customer needs to know.

Where It Ships

Three surfaces. One tool.

Annotated exports from the suite are designed to drop directly into the three highest-traffic content surfaces on B&H's site.

  • Product Detail Pages — hotspot-annotated hero images surface features at the moment of purchase decision
  • Image Galleries — measurement and callout overlays give customers a richer look at gear without leaving the gallery
  • Explora (Blog) — editorial teams use infographic exports to visually explain products inside review and how-to content

Still actively developing

Currently in development with B&H's content and merchandising teams. Next up: bulk export, CMS integration, and a saved annotation library.
