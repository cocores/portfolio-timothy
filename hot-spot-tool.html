The Problem

B&H sells complex, high-consideration gear — cameras, lenses, lighting rigs, audio equipment. Customers browsing a Product Detail Page or Explora article often can't tell what they're looking at without context. Specs live in a table. Features live in a paragraph. But the product sits in a static image with no explanation.

The existing options — custom dev work per image, manual graphic design, or leaving it to copy — were too slow and too expensive to scale across a catalog of thousands.

"The gap wasn't content — it was surface. Customers are already looking at the image. Put the annotation there."

What It Does

Upload any product image and layer annotations directly on top — then export a finished image ready to drop into PDPs, Image Galleries, and Explora editorial content. No design software. No engineering ticket.

Hotspots

Pin numbered callouts to specific product features. Customers tap or hover to reveal details right on the image — bridging what they see and what they need to know.

Measurements

Overlay precise dimension annotations on product images — critical for gear where size and form factor drive purchase decisions.

Infographics

Layer highlight zones and visual callouts to turn a product photo into a feature breakdown — ideal for Explora editorial content.

Callouts

Add labeled arrows pointing to controls, ports, or features — giving customers a richer look at gear without leaving the image.

Where It Ships

Annotated exports from the suite are designed to drop directly into the three highest-traffic content surfaces on B&H's site.

  • Product Detail Pages — hotspot-annotated hero images surface features at the moment of purchase decision
  • Image Galleries — measurement and callout overlays give customers a richer look at gear
  • Explora (Blog) — editorial teams use infographic exports to visually explain products inside review and how-to content

In Development
Next up: bulk export, CMS integration, and a saved annotation library

What I'm Exploring

  • Bulk export — process multiple product images in a single session and download as a ZIP
  • CMS integration — direct publish from the tool into B&H's content management system
  • Annotation library — save and reuse annotations across images in the same product family
  • Measurement accuracy — calibration tools to ensure dimensions reflect real-world product specs
  • Team collaboration — shared workspaces for content and merchandising teams to annotate together
  • Embed format — interactive hotspot layers that render natively on PDPs without a static export
