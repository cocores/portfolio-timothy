Polaroid cameras capture something modern smartphones can't — the feeling of a moment, not just the image. PolaPocket brings that feeling to your phone with instant film filters, physical print framing, and the satisfying imperfection of analog photography.

The Problem

Smartphone cameras are technically perfect — and emotionally flat. Every photo is overexposed, noise-reduced, and AI-sharpened into a version of reality that looks great and feels like nothing. The texture, warmth, and unpredictability that made polaroids special has been optimized away.

People still want that feeling. Disposable camera apps consistently top the App Store. Polaroid film sales have been growing year over year. The demand is there — the tool just hasn't caught up.

"Perfect photos are everywhere. A photo that feels like something is rare."

The Idea

PolaPocket is a camera app that makes your phone shoot like a polaroid — complete with the white frame border, film grain, light leaks, and the slight color drift that makes instant photography feel alive. Shoot, shake, watch it develop. Share the result as a photo or a short video of the development process.

How It Works

Open & Shoot

Open PolaPocket, point your camera, and tap the shutter. The viewfinder previews the polaroid frame and film grain in real time — so what you see is what you get.

Watch It Develop

After the shot, the photo "develops" on screen — starting white and slowly revealing the image with the color gradation and warmth of real instant film. You can shake to develop faster, just like the real thing.

Customize the Frame

Add a caption to the white border below the image — a date, a place, a note — just like writing on the back of a polaroid. Choose from different film stock styles: classic white, colored borders, or vintage yellowed frames.

Share the Moment

Export as a static image or as a short video of the develop animation. Made to share — the video format performs well as a story or reel, and the still works anywhere.

In Development

What I'm Exploring

  • Film stock variety — multiple polaroid styles (Spectra, 600, SX-70) with distinct color profiles and grain
  • Develop animation — a satisfying real-time reveal that rewards the moment of capture
  • Light leaks & imperfections — controlled randomness that makes each photo feel unique
  • Caption & framing — handwriting-style font options for the white border caption
  • Roll view — a gallery that displays photos as a spread of polaroids on a surface
  • Social sharing — native share sheet with the develop video as the default format
