The Problem

A valuable program that no one could find

B&H has offered business financing for years — competitive rates, flexible terms, and access to world-class gear. But the program was invisible. Customers browsing product pages had no clear path to apply, and internal staff weren't consistently surfacing it either.

  • Leasing information was fragmented across multiple pages with no clear entry point
  • The Product Detail Page — where purchase decisions happen — had no financing CTA
  • Internal awareness was low, meaning sales and support teams weren't driving applicants
  • Application drop-off was high due to a multi-step, friction-heavy flow

The insight: This wasn't a demand problem. Customers buying $5K+ gear absolutely want financing options. It was a discovery and experience problem — the program existed, but the path to it didn't.

What I Did

One path. Right where the decision happens.

I led discovery, strategy, and execution to centralize the leasing program onto the Product Detail Page — the highest-intent surface in B&H's catalog. Rather than asking customers to navigate to a separate financing hub, we brought the application to them.

The redesigned experience surfaces financing options contextually alongside the product, with a streamlined single-page application flow and clear lender selection. Internal tooling was also updated so staff could reference and recommend the program confidently.

The Result

225% more applications. $11.1M in new revenue.

Moving financing to the PDP and simplifying the application path removed the discovery barrier entirely. Customers who would have never found leasing on their own were now seeing it exactly when they needed it — at the moment of decision.

Application volume surged by 225%

Driving $11.1M in new annual revenue — from a program that already existed. No new product, no new budget. Just better discovery and a frictionless path to apply.
